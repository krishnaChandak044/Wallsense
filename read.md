# WallSense project notes

The active implementation guide and reproducible setup are in [README.md](README.md). The day-by-day plan, dataset notes, benchmark results, and credit policy are in [doc/](doc/).

WallSense currently evaluates **recorded CSI**. The Day 1–2 results are baselines with explicit limits; they do not establish live or through-wall sensing, clinical fall detection, or vital-sign measurement. Keep the Wallhack and CSI-Bench pipelines separate and preserve those limits in the product and demo.
